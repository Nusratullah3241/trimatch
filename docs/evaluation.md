## 6. Failure analysis

No classification failures on the evaluated sets.

This result should be read narrowly. The test corpus is synthetic, generated by a script with known values, and every document is a digital PDF. The evaluation demonstrates that the pipeline handles format variation and applies its rules correctly; it does not demonstrate robustness to real-world document quality.

Known untested conditions: scanned or photographed documents, rotated or skewed pages, handwritten annotations, multi-page invoices, partial deliveries across several GRNs, and foreign currency.


## Limitations

- Test documents are synthetic and digitally generated. Real scanned invoices with skew, noise, and handwriting would extract less reliably.
- Four vendor layouts were tested. Production would encounter many more.
- Tolerance thresholds (2% price drift, 500 PKR absolute floor) are assumptions, not derived from any real company's approval policy.
- The manual baseline of 12 minutes per set is an industry estimate, not a measured comparison against a specific accounts team.
- Duplicate-invoice detection is excluded from these figures, as noted in section 3.
- The cold timing is sampled from 3 sets, not all of them, because of API quota limits. It is indicative rather than precise.
